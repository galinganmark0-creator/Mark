Customer Support — Voice, Chat & Email Workflows

📌 Voice & Non-Voice Standard Operating Process:

  1. 1. Inbound/Outbound Voice Calls: Build instant rapport, de-escalate callers, and maintain high QA/CSAT scores while meeting daily call volume targets.
  2. 2. Live Chat Handling: Manage concurrent chat sessions with quick first-response times, professional tone, and clear troubleshooting steps.
  3. 3. Email Helpdesk Resolution: Write clear, well-structured email responses (Zendesk/Freshdesk/Gmail) that resolve inquiries in 1 reply to minimize ticket ping-pong.
  4. 4. CRM Logging & Escalation: Document call/chat notes in CRM, tag ticket issues accurately, and escalate complex technical tickets.

💡 Sample Scenario: Email / Chat Refund Request

Approach: Customer requests order cancellation via email -> Verify order status in CRM -> Process refund per company SOP -> Reply with friendly confirmation email & refund receipt in under 15 minutes.

Administrative VA — Systems & Workflows

📌 Executive Support Standard Operating Process:

  1. 1. Inbox Zero Management: Categorize incoming emails into Action Required, Awaiting Reply, or Archive to prevent missed communications.
  2. 2. Calendar & Meeting Prep: Schedule calls with timezone conversions, set 15-minute buffers between meetings, and prepare pre-meeting notes.
  3. 3. Transcription & Documentation: Draft pass -> Timestamping unclear audio -> Context and grammar proofreading to maintain 98% accuracy.
  4. 4. Data Entry & Task Tracking: Keep CRM records updated and manage daily task lists using Trello/Asana/Notion.
